Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what the Solbay game ("Solbay", "we") keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it. Solbay is made by Ryan Romal (United Kingdom), who is responsible for your data. Contact: ryan@romalempire.com.

What we keep

  • Your guest account: when you first play, the game makes an account for you automatically. We keep its account id. We do not ask for your real name, email address or phone number.
  • Your character and progress: your character's name and look, your game save (money, items, home, skills, missions and similar game data), your friends, crew and blocked players.
  • What you write: chat messages and texts you send to other players, and reports you send or that are sent about you.
  • Purchases: which in-app purchases you made, with Apple's transaction id, so we can deliver them and restore them. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • Play and safety data: when you play (sessions), your app version and device type, a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, and game events, used to keep the game fair and stop cheating.

Why we keep it

Only to run the game: to save your progress, let you play and talk with other players, deliver and restore purchases, keep players safe (filtering, reports, blocks) and stop cheating. Our legal basis is the contract to provide the game to you, and our legitimate interest in keeping it fair and safe.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

Who else handles it

Our hosting providers store and process the data for us, under contract: Supabase (database and accounts) and Cloudflare (game servers). Apple processes your purchases under Apple's own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

  • Chat messages: 14 days.
  • Game events used for fairness: 30 days.
  • Your account, character, save and purchases: as long as your account exists.

Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time in the game: start screen > Account > Delete account, or phone > Settings > Delete account. This deletes your account and the data listed above that belongs to it. Your name is removed from shared places such as the Hall of Masters. Deleting your account does not cancel a Solbay Club subscription; cancel that in your Apple Account settings first.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, by emailing ryan@romalempire.com. If you are in the UK or the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Children

Solbay is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep data about children under 13. If you think a child under 13 is playing, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
