SolbayPrivacy Policy

Support

Contact us

Email ryan@romalempire.com. We answer within 2 working days. Please include your character name and your home server.

Report a player

In the game, long-press a chat message or open the player's profile card and choose Report (pick a reason) or Block. We review reports within 24 hours. Blocked players are listed in your phone's Settings, where you can unblock them.

Restore purchases

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Your Starter Pack and Solbay Club come back on the same account.

Solbay Club

Solbay Club is an auto-renewing monthly subscription bought through Apple. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your Apple Account settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions), at least 24 hours before the end of the current month. Refunds for purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account

In the game: start screen > Account > Delete account, or phone > Settings > Delete account. This deletes your account and everything we keep about you. It cannot be undone. Cancel Solbay Club in your Apple subscriptions first, because deleting your account does not stop Apple billing.

Lost progress?

Your progress is saved to your guest account on your phone. If you deleted the app or changed phones, email us with your character name and home server and we will help where we can.
